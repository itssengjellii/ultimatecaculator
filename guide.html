Guide

Everything the calculator understands. Select an example to open it in the calculator.

Arithmetic

Add, subtract, multiply and divide. Multiplication and division run before addition and subtraction; brackets override both.

Percentages

A percent after a number divides it by 100. After + or −, it means a share of the number before it.

Powers and roots

Use ^ for powers. Powers stack from the right, and √ or ∛ take roots.

Factorials

n! multiplies every whole number from 1 up to n. Works for whole numbers from 0 to 170.

Absolute value

abs() drops the sign of a number.

Logarithms

log is base 10. ln is base e. Both need a positive number.

Trigonometry

sin, cos and tan follow the angle unit in Settings. Examples here use degrees.

Inverse trigonometry

sin⁻¹, cos⁻¹ and tan⁻¹ return an angle in the current unit.

Constants

π and e are exact to 15 digits. Put a number in front for implicit multiplication.

Scientific notation

Press EE to write a power of ten, as in 1.5E3 for 1500.

Brackets and implicit multiplication

A number next to a bracket or function multiplies it. Missing closing brackets are added for you.

Fractions

Turn on Fraction on the calculator to see exact results like 1/2 instead of 0.5.
